How a roll works

Each element has its own odds. Rarer elements come up less often, and luck bonuses nudge the weighting toward them.

After every element there is a 1 in 8 chance of adding another, up to 30. Four rare elements and four deep bond lines each improve that, up to 1 in 3.

A chain is written as one formula in Hill order: carbon first, then hydrogen, then the rest alphabetically.

Rarity display

Show each roll's rarity as its odds, or as its score. Score is the base odds of every element in the roll added together.

Joke periodic table

Joke elements roll alongside the real ones. With mixing on they can chain with real elements into joke compounds. With it off, a chain is all joke or all real: a joke element that turns up in a chain takes the roll on its own. Your chances of finding them are the same either way.

Sound

A soft tick while the reel spins and a chime on the result. Rarer results get a fuller chime. Sound starts after you press Roll.
