ElElemental

Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Elemental is a free browser game made by Jamiemek. This page explains what the game stores about you and why.

Playing without signing in

If you never sign in, your collection, settings and progress are saved in your browser's own storage on that device only. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

If the game runs into an error, it sends a short report so the problem can be fixed: what went wrong, the game version, and the type of device and browser. These reports aren't linked to you or your account.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets your progress sync between devices and lets your rolls appear on the global leaderboard. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with the game. We use them only to recognise your account. Your email address is never shown to other players.

For signed-in players, the game stores:

  • your account ID and email address, from Google
  • the display name you choose, which other players can see
  • your saved game progress
  • rare rolls you find, with your display name, so they can appear on the global top 10 and in announcements
  • counts of your rolls and rare finds, used to spot cheating
  • a random ID for each device you play on, and a count of the rolls made on it
  • for finds sent to the global top 10, a record of the roll behind them, so the server can check the find is genuine
  • finds that fail that check, and sudden large jumps in your quarks. These are flagged privately to the game's admins

Rolls that reach the global top 10, and some very rare rolls, are announced in the game and may be posted to the Elemental Discord server, showing your display name and the roll.

Where it is stored

Signed-in data is stored with Supabase, the database service the game uses, and sign-in is handled by Google. Announcements and admin alerts are posted to Discord. We don't sell your data, show ads or share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time and keep playing offline. To have your account and everything stored with it deleted, message Jamiemek on the Elemental Discord. Your data will be removed within a month, and your entries taken off the leaderboard. You can also ask for a copy of what's stored about you the same way.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
